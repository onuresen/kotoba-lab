Backup & restore

Everything on this page lives in this browser only — clearing site data ends it. A backup is one .json file holding your deck with its review scheduling, both known lists, and your study history: the way to move it to another browser or get it back.

Restoring merges — it never deletes what's already here. Where the same word exists on both sides, the one you studied more recently wins, so importing an older backup can't undo newer progress, and importing the same file twice does nothing.

Browser-only · no account · no AI. Tokenizer: instant dictionary longest-match, or precise kuromoji (vendored, loads once). Data: Kanjium / EDRDG (CC BY-SA 4.0) + KanjiVG (CC BY-SA 3.0).
